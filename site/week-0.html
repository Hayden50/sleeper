With some strong areas and some weak spots, Chandler's team is right in the middle of the league. It would be surprising to see this team finishing in the bottom few slots while being equally surprising to see a championship run. Realistically, the best case scenario is squeaking into the 6 team playoff and getting a few lucky wins in a row against favored teams. Some factors favor a lucky deep run: having Hayden as a rival and effectively getting 2 easy wins in the regular season could be huge, and the current starting roster has the highest coefficient of variation outside of Purdy Neat at 0.62, mainly driven by QB2 Malik Willis, who we could see getting benched at some point for a more consistent but probably lower ceiling option in Daniel Jones. A high level of variability in a roster can be seen as a bad thing, but when a roster has virtually no depth at WR after first rounder Travis Hunter has pivoted to mainly a DB, and is projected to score in the 8th/9th range over a season, then variability becomes an asset necessary for having a surprising weekend against tougher competition. With that being said, Chandler does have some real bright spots at certain position groups. Josh Allen is a top tier QB1, Chase Brown gets effectively all of the carries for the Bengals, and CeeDee Lamb was just as recently as 2 years ago seen as a possible WR1. If Jaylen Waddle ends up becoming a major factor in Denver and Rome Odunze has a solid year, this team could end up working out surprisingly well.

It seems like the bright spot is supposed to be the running back room, but even that isn't going well, so I can't imagine this team performing consistently throughout the year. A stretch goal would probably be getting a few lucky game wins and finding a spot in the very bottom of the playoffs where anything could happen.
