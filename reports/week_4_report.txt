POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    At this point Brett is the only possible choice for the top power ranked spot. Being the only undefeated team with the highest PF in the league means that there
    should be no doubt that he is the league favorite this year. Once again Kenneth Walker had an absurd week, and with Ja'Marr Chase dealing with a concussion, Tee
    Higgins stepped up to add 26.7 to the board. Barring future RB health issues, there's not much that could stop this team from continuing their winning ways.


===== Solid Playoff Teams ======


2. Scott
    Congrats to King of the Njigbas for the #2 overall power ranking even with a 2-2 record to start the season. While it is sort of happening by default, with every
    team but Brett having lost games, Scott does have the 2nd highest PF in the league and had a nice 160-point win last week over a struggling Saquon My Balls. Even
    with an average week coming out of JSN, the rest of the team stepped up and looked really good. Specifically, Kyren Williams putting up 36.7 is a great sign for
    the future of the roster.


3. Chandler
    Chandler had a huge victory over Goober McGee last week with the top score in the league, which pushes him up to the #3 power ranked spot this week. If he
    wants to continue to climb, he'll have to carry that momentum into next week as he's playing King of the Njigbas. This is definitely the matchup to watch,
    and it'll be exciting to see which version of Josh Allen we get. If he can put up another 40-point game, I like Chandler's chances at taking that #2 power
    ranked spot next week.


4. Jacob
    Unfortunately, losing to Purdy Neat has to come with a minimum 2-spot drop in the power rankings. Not only are the Cookie Wookies now 2-2, they are also 7th in PF.
    There is also the bad news of Rashee Rice scoring 0 before rolling to the sideline with a hamstring injury. Yes, he's off next week, but hamstring injuries are
    always a tough one to see for skill position players. Hopefully this won't do too much to damage his production, but we'll have to wait and see. The good news
    is that the bench is still as strong as ever - putting up 56 points among Stevenson, Likely, Shaheed, and Loveland. With different management choices going into
    last week, we could have seen a 3-1 roster.


5. Siegel
    Tough loss for Siegel, putting up 150.92 in a loss to Chandler, but all in all it was actually a promising performance for the Goober McGee starters. Drake Maye
    had a bit of a comeback with a 26.2 point week and McMillan put up a ridiculous 45.2, so there are certainly some bright spots. Justin should just chalk this up
    to a kicker / defense combo losing him the week and start looking forward to his week 5 matchup vs the Washington Falcons. If McMillan and Bowers continue performing
    how they have been recently, he'll be just fine.


6. Theo
    For all the hype surrounding this team to start the season, there just hasn't been the production to back it up. Yes, Theo is 3-1, but he is 10th in points scored
    and has a lot of struggling pieces. Last week, we saw Downs put up 4.6, Raymond score 2.6, and Dobbins score 7.2. These numbers might work to get a win over
    Hayden's sorry roster, but they aren't scores that instill confidence in a bright playoff future. The main factors that prop this team up in the power rankings
    even with these issues are the good record and the breakout potential of starting both Bijan Robinson and Puka Nacua. If Theo ends up losing a couple more, we
    could see a steep drop.


===== Outside Looking In ======



7. Nathan
    A big bubble game win against Dig in my Boutte means that Nathan has put the Tickle My Kittle roster into a good spot for a previously unlikely playoff push. While
    it's still early in the season, those kinds of wins are what matter come week 14. After really struggling to put up points to start the season, this roster has now
    produced back-to-back 145+ point victories where once again the load was spread among all the players. Eight starters put up double digits, which is a great sign
    for talks of depth / longevity. Real arguments should be made for moving this team up even more in the power rankings, but we'll give it another week or so and
    continue to evaluate.



====== Meh =======

8. Curran
    Huge win for the Washington Falcons to end the Cinderella story that was the GBGs. Even with another unlucky points against week, the team put up great points
    to secure the victory, and will be bumped up 1 spot as a result. While it was pretty unfortunate to see Ja'Marr Chase go out early with a concussion, the rest
    of the team stepped up: Monangai put up 28, Doubs 23.8, and LaPorta 22.4. At 2-2, if Curran can pull out a win this weekend against a tough Goober McGee, then
    there is a real argument to be made that he deserves a spot in the playoffs.


9. Matt
    Losing the on-the-bubble game last week is a tough look for Dig in my Boutte, who has now dropped to 1-3 and is going to have to slide a bit in the power rankings.
    While Nico Collins (30.8) and Joe Burrow (23.7) tried their best, the RBs really screwed the team over. This roster really needs Breece to come back strong for
    any hope of a bounce back and a lower seeded playoff run.



10. Caleb
    Tough loss for Pitts and Giggles even after putting up his best score of the season. Flowers came back looking great, and the running backs have looked
    consistently good. One bad area is still the TE position, with Kincaid just putting up 1.7, which is a really rough look after trading away Golden and a
    first to acquire him just a couple of weeks ago. If Caleb wants to move up in the power rankings and push towards a playoff spot, he needs to get the tight ends
    to focus up and start scoring at their projected levels.



===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====


11. Darren
    Darren's lucky streak finally caught up to him. Good news for him, Charles' unlucky streak has continued, and it's time to move Charles down where he belongs. With
    that being said, a 3-1 GBGs deserves a delayed bump up in the power rankings after receiving its first quality loss. There are some truly good players on the
    roster with bright futures (Shough, Judkins, Burden, Nabers), so if Darren ends up trading some picks to shore up some depth issues - particularly at RB - then
    maybe they could push up another spot or two.


12. Charles
    This just hasn't been the year for Saquon My Balls. The team can't seem to put it together on any level: Saquon has looked really rough and is dealing with a hamstring
    injury, Herbert is having a huge backslide, and DJ Moore has had constant injury issues that keep him sidelined or only partially participating. At this point in Saquon's
    career, it's not a good sign to deal with nagging things like a hamstring. Hopefully this isn't a sign of a dark future, but I wouldn't be surprised if we just don't see
    great production from him even after recovery. Similarly, these may be signs of a dark future for Charles' team, as now he's stuck with a bad group of guys that
    are also old and have no future.


13. Hayden
    Things are starting to look worse and worse for this team. The "good" players like Hampton and Jefferson may actually suck, and that's a really bad sign when
    the roster has a lot of bad players. Currently Hayden holds sole possession of last place, so maybe if things continue this way we'll actually have to move
    him down another spot in the power rankings.


14. Jesse
    Even though Purdy Neat did pull out the biggest upset of the year, he did it while only scoring 87 points. With that sort of output, the best Jesse is going to get
    is the moral victory and the comfort of knowing that this was the greatest thing that one of his rosters has done since the conception of the league (rivaled only
    by his 2025 toilet bowl finals upset). An additional comfort for this roster is having Hayden's first-round pick, so getting a surprise win in a heavy tanking
    season shouldn't disrupt any plans.
