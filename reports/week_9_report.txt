POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    LeGibbs continued their winning ways with a 150.16-point performance over Nathan and a 157.28-point performance over Darren. At 8-1 with
    the most PF in the league and the best all-play record (90-27), there's really nothing to debate here. Kenneth Walker (36.3) and Jordan Love
    (36.2) carried week 9, and Tee Higgins put up 37.5 in week 8. The only thing to watch for is the injury list - Walker, Evans, and Woody Marks are
    all questionable, and next up is the hottest team in the league (Goober McGee).


2. Siegel
    The trade for Henry and Warren is starting to pay off, and Goober McGee is on fire. The team put up the highest score of week 8 (169.82) 
    and followed it with 149.0 to beat Caleb, and is now 2nd in PF. Brock Bowers (28.2, 22.3) and Christian Watson (17.3, 30.1) have been great. 
    Josh Jacobs is now out, but the RB room is still very solid. Week 10 against Brett is the biggest game of the season so far - a win
    there and Siegel has a real argument for the top spot.


===== Solid Playoff Teams ======

3. Jacob
    Cookie Wookies are on a 4-game win streak after beating Hayden (127.86) and Chandler (148.18), and are tied for 2nd at 6-3. The
    lineup decisions are improving too, with 98% and 92% starter efficiency over the two weeks. The injury report is still long,
    with St. Brown, Garrett Wilson, Stevenson, and Montgomery all questionable, but this team is deep enough to handle it. Next up is Nathan.


4. Chandler
    Chandler split the two weeks, beating Charles 138.86 and then losing to Jacob 148.18 to 118.98. Lineup decisions cost him in both games (82% and 78%
    efficiency). In week 8, Penix started and scored -1.9 while Willis put up 14.6 on the bench. In week 9, Darren Waller put up 23.0 on the bench. CeeDee Lamb finally had a 
    big game (33.5), but it might be too little too late with Waddle out and Willis on IR. There isn't much room for mistakes. Still, the team is 6-3 and looks like it 
    should have a comfortable route to the playoffs.


5. Matt
    Golden Showers have scored the most points in the league over the last two weeks (150.60 and 179.64) and are now 3rd in PF.
    Burrow (39.5, 29.5), Matthew Golden (20.9, 32.4), and Bucky Irving (21.0, 28.5) are all producing, and Breece is back on the field. The success of Braelon Allen while Breece
    was sidelined raised some questions about what the split was going to look like when Breece returned, but it seems like he's definitely retained his RB1 team status. The record 
    is still 4-5, which is the only reason he isn't higher. With the 3rd-highest PF for tiebreakers, Matt might be the most dangerous team in the playoff race, but he still has to 
    win the games to get there.


6. Scott
    Two weeks ago Scott was a Champions-tier team on a 5-game win streak. Since then he lost 150.60 to 114.36 against Matt and 131.20 to 74.58 against Theo,
    and lost Lamar Jackson to IR. Getting Kirk Cousins plus Hayden's 2027 3rd and 4th for Fannin was a nice trade, but Cousins only put up 12.6 in his first start. Now, though, Cousins is 
    heading to the Vikings, so we'll have to see what that does to his production. Scott's all-play record (78-39) is still one of the best in the league, so this is more of 
    a slump than a collapse. Next up is Caleb, who is also struggling, so it's a good chance to get back on track.


===== Outside Looking In ======

7. Curran
    The Washington Falcons have now won three straight and sit at 5-4. Week 8 was a 135.90 blowout of Purdy Neat (Allgeier 36.1,
    Pollard 28.7, Chase 31.1), and week 9 was a 113.16 to 110.62 squeaker over Charles with 99% lineup efficiency. Ja'Marr Chase has been huge (31.1, 31.5),
    but the all-play record (51-66) says this team has been winning a little more than it's been scoring (a big change after being the unluckiest team in the league through the first 
    5ish weeks). The RB injuries are still a problem: Pollard is questionable and Monangai is out.


8. Theo
    Theo went 1-1 over the last two weeks. He lost 169.82 to 106.84 to Siegel, then beat Scott 131.20 behind Puka Nacua (35.0) and Bijan (26.7).
    The bad news is that Puka is now on IR, and this team was already 10th in PF with an all-play record of 44-73. A week 10 matchup against Purdy Neat
    is the best chance he'll get to stay above .500. There's a chance that this team continues to slide.


9. Caleb
    It has been a rough two weeks for Pitts and Giggles. He scored 87.16 (a win, thanks to Darren somehow scoring even less) and 80.88 in a loss to Siegel, and dropped from 6th.
    Pickens put up 6.8 and then 0.0 and is now questionable, and both QBs combined for under 24 in week 9. DeMario Douglas scored 16.6 and 15.2 on the bench. At 5-4 he's still
    in the race, but the scoring has to come back fast. The goal at this point should be to find a way to sneak into the back of the playoff group.


====== Meh =======

10. Nathan
    Two weeks ago I had Tickle My Kittle in full rebuild mode, but a 135.44 win over Hayden (thanks to C.J. Stroud's 35.4) gets Nathan out of the
    rebuilder tier for now. The week 8 loss to Brett (150.16 to 108.68) was expected. At 4-5 with the 7th-highest PF, this is a respectable team, but Jacob in week 10 
    is looking rough.


11. Charles
    Saquon My Balls lost both games: 138.86 to 109.08 against Chandler and a 113.16 to 110.62 heartbreaker against Curran. In that one, Justice Hill (18.2) and Courtland Sutton (15.5)
    sat on the bench while Geno (7.4) and Addison (4.8) started, so a better lineup wins that game. The all-play record (42-75) is still among the worst in the league. Week 10 is Matt, 
    which is bad news with how Golden Showers has been performing recently.


===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====

12. Darren
    The GBGs dropped both games and fall back to 4-5. Week 8 was ugly (74.90 with Will Howard at QB for 0.0 and Adonai Mitchell scoring 27.2 on the bench), and week 9
    was a respectable 119.34 loss to Brett. Shough (19.7) and Burden (20.7) look like real building blocks, but with Hurts on IR, Tate out, and Burden questionable,
    this is a thin roster. Week 10 against Hayden is about as good a matchup as you can get. Unfortunately, with the Thursday game already played at the time of writing this,
    Hayden is actually projected to win. This could be an embarrassing week for the GBGs.


13. Jesse
    Purdy Neat put up 45.50 in week 8, the lowest score of the season, after starting Purdy, Tre Tucker, and Godwin, who all scored 0.0 (60% efficiency). Week 9 was
    better at 71.96, but that was still a 107-point loss to Matt. At 1-8 with the lowest PF in the league, the main thing to watch is Hayden's 2027 first, which looks
    better every week.


14. Hayden
    0-9. Hayden traded Kirk Cousins and his 2027 3rd and 4th to Scott for Harold Fannin, which is about as clear a "we're rebuilding" signal as you can send. Week 8 was
    a 127.86 to 109.48 loss to Jacob with 71% lineup efficiency, after starting Jake Ferguson (0.0) while Cam Ward (26.3) and Gesicki (17.6) sat on the bench. Week 9 was 71.40, the
    lowest score of the week. Hayden plays Darren next, so this might be the best chance left at a win this season. With Purdy Neat really struggling, a win against the GBGs could possibly
    move Hayden back up to 13th in the power rankings.
