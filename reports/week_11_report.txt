POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    LeGibbs put up the highest score of the week (163.36) to beat Scott, and did it with 100% lineup efficiency. The new guys paid off right away: DJ Moore
    put up 23.1 and George Kittle added 18.4, right alongside Gibbs (27.8), Emmett Johnson (24.3), and Evans (22.9). At 10-1, Brett has officially clinched
    a playoff spot with a bye. Walker is still out and Woody Marks is questionable, but after last week's trades that doesn't
    really matter anymore. Next up is Purdy Neat, which reminds me of the classic SEC cupcake week at the end of the season.


2. Jacob
    Cookie Wookies beat Curran 134.54 to 107.96 and have now won six straight. Travis Kelce (25.4) and James Cook (17.2) led the way, and it could have been
    even more with Montgomery (15.4) and Loveland (11.9) on the bench. At 8-3 with a 103-40 all-play record (2nd in the league), the playoffs are effectively a lock. 
    The only real question left is if Jacob can grab a bye. With a win over the GBGs this week (probable), Jacob's bye chances go up to 82%. With a loss, they drop to 46%.


===== Solid Playoff Teams ======

3. Matt
    I said last week that the Chandler matchup was a must-win, and Golden Showers delivered with 149.40 (2nd-highest of the week). McConkey
    (20.7), Burrow (19.9), Irving (19.0), Breece (19.0), and Parker Washington (18.3) were all solid. That's four straight wins, and Matt has passed Jacob for
    the 2nd-most PF in the league (1555.38). That win moved his playoff odds from 64% to 90%, and his 156.0 average over the last three weeks is 3rd-best
    in the league. Next week against Curran, a win puts him at 97.5% to make the playoffs. A loss drops that to 73%.


4. Chandler
    Chandler lost 149.40 to 113.66 to Matt, and, even worse, it wasn't really a management problem (95% efficiency). CeeDee Lamb (30.2) and Hubbard
    (26.0) had good performances, but Josh Allen (12.2), Goedert (4.8), and the Bucs defense (0.0) didn't get it done. Last week I said a loss would drop his bye chances
    to 13%, and now they're down to 9% with other matchups coming into play. The good news is that the playoffs are still very likely at 96.5%. Theo is up next, and a win 
    there makes it 99.5%. Notably, Tuten and Omar Cooper are questionable, so hopefully they make a quick recovery before the playoffs.


5. Caleb
    Pitts and Giggles did what they were supposed to do, beating Purdy Neat 127.54 to 79.22 behind Jeanty (27.9) and Pickens (23.8). At 7-4, his playoff odds
    are now up to 82%. The PF (7th) and all-play record (75-68) still say this team has been lucky, but at the end of the day those stats don't really matter if you 
    keep winning. A big game against a tanking Charles is next. A win puts him at 93% to make the playoffs, and a loss drops him to 62%. Marvin Harrison Jr., Kincaid, and Carson Beck are
    all questionable, so the injury list is getting long, which has been a concern all season for this roster.


6. Siegel
    Last week I said the Hayden matchup should help Goober McGee get some momentum back. Instead, Siegel scored 99.46 and became the latest victim of the
    Hayden revenge tour. Lineup decisions hurt again (80% efficiency). Juwan Johnson started at TE and scored 0.0 while Marlin Klein (12.4) sat, and
    Croskey-Merritt (15.7) was on the bench while Warren (4.9) and Keon Coleman (3.7) started. Over the last three weeks, Siegel is averaging 108.9, which is
    8th in the league over that stretch. His playoff odds dropped from 91% to 73%. Next week against Nathan is basically a playoff game. A win puts Siegel
    at 90%, and a loss drops him to 46%.


===== Outside Looking In ======

7. Scott
    Scott had a brutal 163.36 to 88.68 loss to Brett, his fourth straight loss, and it was the worst lineup in the league this week (72% efficiency). Cousins (7.4) and
    Rachaad White (0.0) started while Jack Bech (22.8) and Brissett (13.7) sat on the bench, and Shrader put up 1.4 at kicker. I said last week that a
    loss would put Scott's playoff chances around 44%. It's actually a little worse now at 37% with everyone else's results. The all-play record (86-57) still says this is a better team
    than 5-6, but at some point the results have to show up. The good news is that next up is Hayden, but we said that about Siegel last week. A win gets him to 45%, but a loss would knock 
    him down to 10%.


8. Curran
    The Washington Falcons lost 134.54 to 107.96 to Cookie Wookies. Dak was great again (35.9), but Chase (8.1), Skattebo (5.8), and Monangai (1.8) weren't, and
    Worthy (11.8) sat on the bench. Curran has the most points against in the league (1473.54), so the bad luck is still a theme. At 5-6 he's at 8%
    to make the playoffs, so his playoffs basically start right now. Even winning out might not be enough - in the sims where Curran wins all three, he only gets in 81% of the time. A win puts him at 25%, and a loss drops him to 1.4%.


9. Nathan
    Last week I said a loss to the GBGs could send Nathan down to the rebuilder tier. Instead, The Meddlers (formerly Tickle My Kittle)
    won 107.56 to 73.48, led by Jonah Coleman (34.9). It wasn't pretty, with 82% efficiency, Tyler Bass (17.7) on the bench while Aubrey (10.9) started, and
    Metcalf (1.2), Quentin Johnston (2.0), and Michael Mayer (1.4) not doing much. At 5-6 he's still alive at 11%, and the schedule (Siegel, Scott, Jesse)
    is pretty favorable. This week against Siegel is the big one, as Nathan is in a very similar spot to the rest of the Outside Looking In tier - they need to win every game left. A win gets 
    Nathan to 28%, and a loss drops him to 0.6%.


====== Meh =======

10. Charles
    Saquon My Balls beat Theo 114.68 to 110.84 behind Javonte Williams (24.1), Herbert (22.4), and Geno (19.4), and is somehow 5-6 even after selling DJ Moore. An argument could be 
    made that this result is disappointing for Charles, seeing as the playoffs are out of reach while the draft pick keeps getting later. Justice Hill (17.6) was on the 
    bench again, just like week 9. Even at 5-6, the PF (11th) puts his playoff odds at just 1%, and Herbert is questionable
    with a concussion. Next up is Caleb, and even with a win, Charles only goes up to 2.7%. At this point, the most likely way this team affects the playoffs
    is by beating Caleb and giving other teams an opportunity to slip in.


11. Theo
    Theo lost 114.68 to 110.84 to Charles. Mahomes (25.0) and Baker (19.1) had a good day, but Dobbins (0.0) and Etienne (7.6) didn't, and Vele (14.6) sat
    on the bench. That's two straight losses, and with Puka on IR and Dobbins now out, it's doomed. Theo's playoff odds are down to 1.4%, and
    next up is Chandler. This is looking like a toilet bowl team.


===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====

12. Darren
    The GBGs scored a horrendous 73.48, the lowest score of the week, and lost to Nathan for their fourth straight loss. The 75% efficiency means the blame is on management
    just as much as it's on the mediocre players. Ted Hurst III put up 22.6 on the bench, while the starting RBs (Jaydn Ott and Jaylen Wright) combined for 2.2. Darren also 
    dropped Ott for Max Hurleman on waivers, then picked Ott back up as a free agent and started him anyway. Last week's 0.5% playoff chance is now 0%. The loss streak might 
    continue as Darren is up against Jacob next week.


13. Hayden
    The revenge tour is real. Hayden beat Siegel 120.52 to 99.46 for back-to-back wins. Cam Ward (22.2), the Bears defense
    (16.0), and Perine and Jefferson (13.7 each) carried it, and that's with Fernando Mendoza putting up 22.9 on the taxi squad. With both teams at 2-9,
    Hayden moves past Purdy Neat in the power rankings. Hayden is eliminated, so the only goal left is playing spoiler. Next is Scott, whose playoff odds drop from 45% to 10% if
    Hayden wins. After that is Purdy Neat in week 13, and since Jesse owns Hayden's 2027 1st, every win hurts that first pick a bit more.


14. Jesse
    Purdy Neat lost 127.54 to 79.22 to Caleb Purdy (22.2) and Slye (14.6) performed fine, but Antonio Williams scored 21.2 on the bench.
    At 2-9 with the lowest PF in the league, Jesse is mathematically eliminated and drops back to the bottom after Hayden's win. The good news is that none of this matters since
    this is where the team expected to be at this point anyway. The 2030 season is going to be awesome for these guys.
