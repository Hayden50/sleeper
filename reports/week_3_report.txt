POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    After putting up nearly 60% of Darren's season total in one game, it's probably time to move 
    FredJHCJ09 into the #1 spot on the power rankings. Not only are his 2 running backs #1 and #2 in their position group,
    they are also both in the top 5 scorers overall. If they continue this level of consistency for the rest of the season
    then it should be no surprise if Brett ends up going all the way. One thing to keep in mind is that concerns with bench
    depth are still very relevant, and an injury to a key player (in this case one of the RBs) could do huge damage to this
    team. Unlike other top teams, Brett doesn't have a lot of high level startable pieces waiting for a spot on the starting
    roster. The closest thing to a high level startable bench player he has is Mike Evans who is already dealing with issues
    related to a cracked rib. Maybe RJ Harvey can be a suitable replacement for a starting runningback if necessary, but last
    year he was notably inconsistent.


2. Jacob
    Cookie Wookies will be moving down to the #2 power rankings even after a solid win against Pitts and Giggles not due to their
    own performance but just because Brett has looked so dominant to start the season. Looking forward to next week, this team
    is facing off against Purdy Neat, so I'd expect an easy victory to push to 3-1 (81% win probability when 0 players have played
    is insane). One piece of good news for Jacob is that McCaffrey bounced back well after a rough week 1, and had a 30% boost in
    snap percentage in the most recent game vs the first 2 games, so he should look out for a classic CMC boom game coming soon.


===== Deep Playoff Runs ======


3. Scott
    Scott has had a sneaky good season so far this year. Having an unlucky 0-2 start had slid him down a bit in the power
    rankings, but it's time to bring things back up after a clean 57.2 point victory over Purdy Neat. JSN is playing unbelievably
    well this year and if he can maintain his spot as the #1 player in fantasy, Scott could be a dangerous team.


4. Chandler
    Not much to say for Chandler this week. He got hit with the GBGs curse and dropped a game, but we can chalk it up to a really
    bad performance by the kicker / defense and move on. For now we'll keep him in the #4 power ranking spot and see if 
    Josh Allen gets back to those 40 point games he had to start the season.


5. Siegel
    An unfortunate loss last week won't hurt Goober McGee for now, mostly because of the performance Brock Bowers put 
    up in his first week back. Last week I mentioned that this team was supposed to struggle through the first couple
    of weeks while they waited to get to full strength with Bowers and Jacobs. Now that Bowers is back and is confirmed 
    looking like himself - putting up 27.6 with 14 targets - I am confident that there will be a bounce back from this 
    loss. One thing to look out for is the QB situation. Drake Maye has significantly struggled to start the season, and is 
    yet to crest 10 points. If that continues, Siegel might be forced to play Deshaun Watson. 


6. Theo
    A combination of continued injury issues and poor performance to start the season is going to move Theo down to 
    6th in the power rankings. Although he had a good week 1, putting up 101 and 102 in the last 2 weeks is a bad sign
    for the rest of the season. If Theo would have played Purdy Neat the last 2 weeks, he would have lost both of those
    games. On top of the low scores, he now has to deal with Mayfield and Etienne both being out along with Coker and 
    Nacua still being limited. There is a world where Theo loses again this upcoming week, and in that case I would 
    expect this team to continue to slide in the power rankings.


===== Outside Looking In ======


7. Matt
    Not much to say for Matt this week. He had a solid game against a bad opponent and produced an expected win. Injuries
    to Breece Hall and a questionable Bucky Irving aren't great, but a returning Nico Collins means this is kind of a wash. 
    Dig in my Boutte plays Tickle My Kittle this upcoming week, which should be an interesting game. Currently these 2 teams
    are power ranked next to each other on the playoff bubble, so this could be a crucial matchup to determine who makes it in 
    and who is left out later this season.


8. Nathan
    I may have given up hope on Nathan's team too quickly, and will be moving them up in the power rankings after just 
    one week in the Meh tier. What really impressed me about the Tickle My Kittle squad was that 6 of his starters scored
    over 20 points in their 187.62 point blowout. There are a significant amount of teams in the league (mostly ranked 
    below this) that realistically have no chance of an outcome like that happening. Maybe they could have 2 or 3 players 
    have huge performances to push up towards the 180 mark, but carrying the load between the whole team means that Nathan
    has a lot of guys that could boom week to week.


====== Meh =======

9. Curran
    Curran got hit doubly hard this week losing Achane for the season and losing to a team power ranked below him by 84. It seems
    like luck hasn't been on his side, giving up the most points so far this season (473.98) while also having to go through
    some big injuries for his better players (Daniels, Achane). There are bright spots to look forward to though with Ja'Marr
    Chase bouncing back after a disappointing week 1, and this week having the opportunity to end the GBGs undefeated 
    season. Hopefully Curran can right the ship and get back to 2-2.


10. Charles
    A good bounce back win against Siegel will move Saquon My Balls up a spot in the power rankings, but that doesn't mean 
    this team is in the clear. There are still a lot of issues going on that have me hesitant on whether or not any sort
    of push could be made with this squad. The major issue I see is that the players who are supposed to be the big scorers 
    just aren't getting it done to start the season. Unfortunately, Herbert (and the Chargers in general) has looked very rough,
    and Saquon hasn't scored in the double digits all year. This combined with a lack of depth (starting Gadsden who put up 0) 
    makes it look like there could be some tough times ahead.


11. Caleb
    Pitts and Giggles will be dropping another spot in the rankings this week after putting up the lowest score in the league. 
    Concerningly, while no one played particularly poorly, there also wasn't any standouts to build some confidence around. My
    biggest piece of advice for this squad is start thinking of some options for a name change. Realistically Kyle Pitts doesn't
    deserve a reference in the team name and there's probably nothing to be giggling about after this start to a season.



===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====


12. Darren
    If Darren's players performed half as well as he seems to think they are performing, his team would be moving up in 
    the power rankings. Unfortunately, this squad really just isn't very good and continues to scrape by in the luckiest
    manner possible. With 324.18 PF, he is less than 3 points away from being the lowest scorer of the entire
    season. This upcoming week the GBGs are matched up against The Washington Falcons, which is a good narrative game as 
    this is probably the luckiest vs unluckiest team. It'll be interesting to see if Curran's streak of giving up 130+ will 
    finally come to an end or if the GBGs will pull out another victory.


13. Hayden
    Not a lot happened for Hayden's team this week. Suffering a blowout against Dig in my Boutte moves him to 0-3 in 
    the league while being 9th in PF. Concerningly, his big stars Omarion Hampton and Justin Jefferson combined for
    just 10.8, which is not what you want to see for a rebuilding team. Hopefully this is a small blip and these bigger
    name players can reset and improve throughout the season. Part of the reason for that low number anyway would be the 
    minor ankle injury for Jefferson that had him sitting out the second half of the game.


14. Jesse
    Jesse actually had the highest points among all losers in the league this past weekend at 118.48. Unfortunately, 
    he was still among the losers, and will be moving down to 0-3 and will remain in last place in the power rankings. 
    As a team that seems to be actively tanking, he should feel good that even though his PF this season is less than 
    2 points below Darren's (321.96 vs. 324.18), he is in dead last while Darren is in second place.
