POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Jacob
    One close loss in week 1 won't move Cookie Wookies in the power rankings. Most likely we will see stronger performances for McCaffrey and Stafford
    moving forward, and Amon Ra St. Brown / Garrett Wilson performed well. The biggest issue is Colston Loveland getting 0 points in a 59 point victory.
    Luckily for Jacob, he has the deepest TE room in the league, and can slot in pretty much whoever he wants there. Likely had a huge
    week 1 performance, so I wouldn't be surprised to see him take the starting spot.

2. Brett
    Big win for LeGibbs this week keeps him at the #2 spot in the power rankings. Having RB#2 and RB#3 in week 1 is insane, and if Kenneth Walker keeps 
    up the performance he had week 1, I might have to move this team up to the #1 spot. Besides the RBs, the rest of the team didn't look that great. Every
    WR underperformed projections. Out of those, the most concerning is probably Drake London, who may be suffering from the QB situation in Atlanta.


===== Deep Playoff Runs ======


3. Theo
    Huge win for Theo this week over the power ranked #1 team. A surprising WR1 performance for Jalen Coker and a less surprising 31 point finish for 
    Bijan Robinson were big factors in the victory. If these guys keep playing at that level, and Puka Nacua turns it around after a boring first game, 
    this team is a strong contender for the championship.


4. Scott
    Scott had an unlucky upset loss to Goober McGee, but there shouldn't be any concerns going into the rest of the season. Every player carried their own
    for the most part, so King of the Njigbas keeps their #4 ranked spot. D'Andre Swift had a great game, with 32 points on just 13 projected, you can't complain,
    but I'd be surprised if we can expect big numbers like that every week. He only had 57% snap percentage, and the Bears did score 59 points against a bad 
    Panthers defense.


5. Curran
    Week 1 was a bit of a tough watch for Curran, whose team is always going to struggle if possibly the best fantasy player in the league (Jamarr 
    Chase) has a bad week, but even still the rest of the team just seemed pretty mediocre. Achane only had 11 carries for 35, Brian Thomas only had 3
    targets, and Doubs had 0 points total. This still isn't a bad roster, but when compared to the other teams in this area, it's not looking as good 
    as I thought it would after week 1.


===== Solid Playoff Teams ======

6. Caleb
    Week 1's top scorer with 162.3 - Caleb's team looked pretty good, with huge performances from his runningbacks. If they keep performing
    like that, they could be league winning players. Struggles in the tight end position and injury scares with Zay Flowers keep me from raising
    him any higher for now.


7. Matt
    Dig in my Boutte's lack of QB depth came to relevance very quickly, with Sam Darnold going down on his first drive of the game. He was able to pick up Drew
    Lock, but being forced to start a perennial backup QB and starting 0-1 will drop him a spot in the power rankings. On top of these issues, Nico Collins and 
    Ladd McConkey are both dealing with injury issues, and haven't been in practice this week. If they don't play, the roster starts getting pretty thin.
    


8. Chandler
    Pretty routine win for Chandler to start the season. Josh Allen and Chase Brown had great games, and Chuba Hubbard could end up being
    pretty legit, which adds yet another piece to the starting lineup. I don't want to overreact to a good week 1 for some of his better players,
    so for now he'll stay where he's at.

====== Meh =======


9. Charles
    Charles' team looked pretty good in his win over Tickle My Kittle. Javonte Williams and Trey McBride
    had a great start, and things look promising in Buffalo for DJ Moore. Herbert had a bad week but he will probably bounce back. The 
    thing that keeps Saquon My Balls down in the standings is a total lack of depth. Being forced to start Rico Dowdle will lose you some
    games this season.

10. Siegel
    Goober McGee had a big comeback to start the season 1-0 off the backs of massive performances from Olave, Watson, and
    Bryce Young, setting him as the second highest scorer in week 1 at 159.76 points. Unfortunately for him, the rest of the 
    team looked kind of mediocre. Drake Maye might not be the QB he was last year now that he's playing real teams, and I don't know if 
    we should expect these huge boom performances from some mid tier players like Watson and Young. For now, he gets a bump up one slot 
    in the rankings.


===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====

11. Nathan
    Good news for Nathan is that Derrick Henry is still a great player. Unfortunately, it seems like the rest of the team 
    might not be great players. Similar to the entire Tickle My Kittle team, Bo Nix had an ugly start to the season that did
    not match last year's performances. Kittle was pretty much invisible in his first game back, and now AJ Brown joins Pearsall
    on IR. Another small note is that the Rams defense didn't really produce what you'd hope for, and now Myles Garrett is dealing
    with injuries himself.

12. Darren 
    Darren's team performed better than expected, with Tyler Shough appearing as a pretty strong QB. This is good for his team,
    but probably bad for his chances of getting Jeremiah Smith. Unfortunately for him, his RBs struggled to get much going.

13. Hayden
    Things were looking good for a second, but a QB matchup against Chandler where Hayden's QB1 put up -0.38 and Chandler's
    QB1 put up 35.66 meant the difference.

14. Jesse
    Nothing to see here. Week 1 went about as expected. Jesse had to decide between Terrance Ferguson and Theo Johnson
    for the tight end position, and he chose correctly with Theo Johnson, who put up 1.9 points. Probably won't win a game
    this year.
