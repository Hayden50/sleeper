POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    Games like what we saw between Curran and Brett this past weekend are why you have to love rivalry week. 
    Anything can happen, and in this case it meant that with a 2.2-point loss to Curran, the win streak is over 
    and the chance of the first Wooglie Boys Dynasty undefeated season is gone. With that being said, one 
    close loss won't move anything for this team in the rankings, and I still expect a deep run in the playoffs.


2. Scott
    Scott is now on a huge 5-game win streak, making him eligible to join Brett up in the Champions tier. Scoring
    the second highest score of the week at 145.86 and taking care of rival Goober McGee, this team is looking like a real 
    threat at this point. Interestingly, Scott's "all play record" (the record you'd have if you played everyone every 
    week) is actually the best in the league at 69-22, which means this is the most consistent
    team in the league. That level of consistency is important as we approach the playoffs. If DeVonta Smith can
    get healthy, there aren't many weaknesses in the roster.

===== Solid Playoff Teams ======

3. Chandler
    A great week for Chandler, putting up the highest score in the league this past week, moves him up 
    to a podium spot on the power rankings. With a 5-2 record and the 3rd highest PF, there aren't many issues
    with this team. Both QBs put up over 20 points and Omar Cooper had a great first week post-injury.



4. Jacob
    A good win last week puts Cookie Wookies back in a solid spot, especially since his upcoming opponent is 
    Hayden. Fortunately for him, this favorable matchup is coming at a time when his team is stretched thin
    with byes and injuries. McCaffrey and Likely are on bye, Montgomery and Shaheed are out, and Adams is still
    on IR. Even with these issues, the team should expect to come out with a win.


5. Siegel
    Goober McGee had a huge opportunity this past week to take a win off of a top team and move up into 
    a real contender spot, but struggled to convert - putting up just 116.46. Combined with concerns about future
    punishment for Josh Jacobs, he is going to have to move down in the rankings.


6. Caleb
    Another win for Pitts and Giggles (and officially sweeping Matt in the 2026 rivalry matchup) means he 
    gets to move back into the solid playoff team category. For the last 3 weeks, he has averaged
    139.3 (third best over that time period). The QB issue is still real though. Daniel Jones in his first week
    on the Pitts and Giggles roster put up just 3.6. The good news is that next week's game is against the GBGs,
    so if Caleb can keep up the high scoring average he should be fine.


===== Outside Looking In ======

7. Theo
    A sub-100-point loss is going to move Theo down into the "Outside Looking In" tier. In the most recent matchup
    there is some blame to be put on the manager, since the team had the lowest starting efficiency in the league
    last week at 73%. Theo is 10th in PF, but has good players, so if he can get the right guys in the lineup in 
    the right weeks, he can still make a push.


8. Curran
    Last week I said I'd expect Curran to lose to Brett and slide to 2-5. Instead, the Washington Falcons handed LeGibbs their first loss of the season, splitting the rivalry series in a 129.38 to
    127.18 thriller. Tony Pollard put up 36.5 as the RB room stepped up without Achane, and Dak added 23.3. Bad luck has been a theme all year for Curran - his 1020.1
    points against is the most in the league by nearly 70 points, and roughly 20 points a week more than the league average. With Monangai out and Skattebo questionable, the injuries are still a real problem,
    but a week 8 matchup against Purdy Neat (with Purdy on bye) means this team has a good chance to get back to .500.


====== Meh =======

    
9. Matt
    This season has to be frustrating for the newly named Golden Showers. Being 6th in PF while sitting at 2-5 is not great, but with such a high PF for tie-breaker scenarios, it's not completely lost yet. At
    this point, each game is a must win. The good news is that Burrow has stayed healthy and is up against the Titans this week, and McConkey is back in the lineup. At the same time, Breece is now questionable and
    could play. If everyone gets back on the field and stays healthy maybe there is a chance.



10. Charles
    Saquon My Balls swept rival Nathan with a 134.48-point win and is suddenly 4-3. Trey McBride (27.1), Geno Smith (23.5), and Javonte Williams (21.5) led the way, and Saquon (15.1) was finally back on the field.
    Charles also continued the sell-off, sending Jacoby Brissett to Scott for a 2028 2nd. That said, Charles is 12th in PF and his all-play record (33-58) is tied for the worst of any team with a winning record, so 
    I'd still keep the sell-off going. Week 8 against Chandler might be tough, but it's a winnable matchup.


11. Darren
    It's time to rethink expectations for the GBGs. Ironically, this team picked up lots of lucky wins to start the season while struggling to score, but now it's dropping
    occasional games while looking way better. Last week was a great performance by the players and management - putting up 135.42 against rival Purdy Neat to 
    complete the sweep, and doing it with 99% lineup efficiency. The one main concern is injuries now that Hurts is out indefinitely and Tate is dealing with his own
    issues.


===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====


12. Nathan
    With the loss to Charles, it seems like Tickle My Kittle is in full rebuild mode. Since trading away Henry and Warren, the team has scored 99.6 and 117.1, and things
    are only going to get worse as AJ Brown and TreVeyon Henderson are both out. On top of that, Dameon Pierce is on IR. One bright spot is that Bo Nix has had some good 
    weeks recently, but next week is against an angry FredJHCJ coming off a loss, so it might be rough.


13. Jesse
    Purdy Neat lost 135.42 to 106.40 to complete the season sweep for Darren, but the optimal lineup scored 141.3. Caleb Douglas (21.0), Malachi
    Fields (12.3), and Kaelon Black (10.9) all sat on the bench while Roschon Johnson (1.3) and MarShawn Lloyd (5.5) started. People are saying this 
    team might be tanking after a management performance like that. One good thing is the week 6 waiver pickup of Mason Taylor, who put up 25.2 at TE.
    Egbuka is out with a foot injury and Purdy is on bye, so week 8 against Curran looks tough.

14. Hayden
    Hayden is the only winless team in the league after a 146.44 to 99.50 loss to rival Chandler, which moves the all-time series to 5-1. Hayden has averaged 88.6 over the last three weeks, and his all-play
    record is 16-75 - he would have lost to almost anyone in almost any week. Kirk Cousins (20.4), Braelon Allen (19.4), and Emanuel Wilson (18.5) made it respectable, but Jefferson (8.4) and Jake Ferguson (0.0) did not.
    For any fans of this roster, there's maybe a cope angle this week with Jacob missing McCaffrey and Likely. Unfortunately, this team might not win a game this season.
