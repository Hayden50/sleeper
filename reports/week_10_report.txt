POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    LeGibbs dominated Goober McGee last week 162.12 to 78.12, nearly winning with just his 2 starting RBs in Gibbs (44.2) and Emmett Johnson (35.1), and
    moved to 9-1. Brett hasn't locked up a playoff spot yet, but at this point it would be shocking if he didn't get a bye. More interesting
    than Brett winning another game was him going all in on winning now: trading for DJ Moore (two 2nds to Charles) and George Kittle (his 2027 1st and a 3rd to Nathan).
    With Walker and Woody Marks out, the timing makes sense if he wants to keep a fully strong starting roster. It also helps with previous concerns about depth.


2. Jacob
    Cookie Wookies put up 190.96 against Nathan, one of the highest scores of the season, with McCaffrey (46.3), Likely (27.7), and St. Brown (25.8) leading the way.
    That's five straight wins, and he's 7-3 with the 2nd-most PF and the 2nd-best all-play record (92-38). While it took some movement in the power rankings due to some
    unfortunate early losses, it's always been clear these were the two best teams.


===== Solid Playoff Teams ======

3. Chandler
    Chandler won a 162.78 to 156.38 shootout with Curran thanks to Hubbard (34.2), Chase Brown (28.3), and Tuten (20.3), and is tied for 2nd at 7-3. When looking at
    future seeding, the extra win that puts Chandler tied for second is a massive boon that allows him to lose one game without too much trouble. Looking at PF, Chandler's
    1379.72 is higher than both of the 6-4 squads, but lower than Golden Showers at 5-5. This means that next week's matchup against Matt has huge playoff implications.
    If Chandler can get another win, his chances of a bye are 43%. If he loses, those drop to just 13%.


4. Matt
    Golden Showers handled Charles 138.96 to 100.58 with 98% lineup efficiency, led by Burrow (33.2) and Parker Washington (28.2). At 5-5 with the 3rd-most PF and an
    84-46 all-play record, this team keeps looking better than its record. Like I mentioned last week, losing so many early games has created more urgency than usual,
    so this matchup against Chandler next week is still a must-win. A loss means Matt would more likely than not miss the playoffs entirely.


5. Siegel
    Two weeks ago Siegel had the chance to take the top spot, and instead put up 78.12 against Brett with 67% efficiency, the worst in the league this week.
    Maye (3.0) and Bryce Young (8.1) started while Deshaun Watson (21.4) and Diggs (20.3) sat on the bench, and now Maye is out. Week 11 against Hayden should help in his
    campaign to get some momentum back, but Goober McGee clearly threw away the strong position he was in. Now he has a team like Golden Showers right behind him that he needs
    to be watching out for.


6. Caleb
    Pitts and Giggles bounced back with 161.90 against Scott, led by Pickens (43.5) and Trevor Lawrence (26.8). At 6-4 he's in a playoff spot, but the PF (8th)
    and all-play record (65-65) say he's been a little lucky. Next up is Jesse, which will be massive for his playoff chances. As long as he can do what he's supposed to do
    and win this week, he has a 74% chance to make the playoffs.


===== Outside Looking In ======

7. Scott
    Scott lost 161.90 to 116.00 to Caleb and has now dropped three straight to fall to 5-5. JSN (31.1) showed up, but not much else did, and Kamara (16.4) sat on
    the bench. The 84-46 all-play record still says this team is better than this, but Brett next week isn't the way to end the slide. Even though this team has shown
    really high highs this season, assuming that this upcoming week is a loss (which isn't a guarantee), Scott's chances of making the playoffs would sit at 44%.


8. Curran
    The Washington Falcons put up the 4th-highest score of the week (156.38) and still lost to Chandler, which feels very on brand for this team. Dak (34.4), Chase (34.1),
    and Skattebo (27.2) were great, but Worthy put up a 0.0 in the lineup. At 5-5, drawing Cookie Wookies next week is tough, and it's a game that Curran really needs
    to win to keep his season hopes alive.


9. Theo
    This team has to be down after an absolutely embarrassing loss last week to Purdy Neat. Looking back, a major reason for the loss was lineup decisions (78% efficiency).
    Mahomes put up 35.4, but Coker (0.3) and Singletary (0.3) started while Downs (15.3) and Keenan Allen (11.1) sat. That upset loss last week might have cost Theo his chances of
    making it out. Even with a win today, it's very unlikely we see this team anywhere but the toilet bowl bracket come postseason.


====== Meh =======

10. Charles
    Saquon My Balls lost 138.96 to 100.58 to Matt, then sold DJ Moore (his top scorer this week at 19.4) to Brett for a 2027 and 2029 2nd. At 4-6 with the 11th-highest PF,
    it's a sensible move. Theo in week 11 is a winnable game, but even with a win the ship has pretty much sailed on this team's playoff chances. Trading away your
    best receiver is a sign that Charles knows it too.


11. Nathan
    Tickle My Kittle got run over by Jacob 190.96 to 76.74, the lowest score of the week, and then traded his namesake George Kittle to Brett for a 2027 1st and 3rd.
    Brett's 1st will probably be near the end of the round, but it's still a decent return for an older player. If nothing else, this next week against the GBGs has large
    implications for the power rankings. With a loss I could see Nathan joining the other bottom feeders in the Future Dynasty / Rebuilders tier.


===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====

12. Darren
    Last week I said this could be an embarrassing week for the GBGs, and it was. Darren scored 84.58 and handed Hayden his first win of the season. At 4-6 with
    Hurts on IR, it's probably time to lean into the future. For the GBG believers out there, a win this week gives the team a 0.5% chance to make the playoffs, and
    everyone knows if you're gonna win it all, it starts with making the playoffs. It's time to take the ping pong tables out of the locker room and lock in for the rest
    of the season.


13. Jesse
    Purdy Neat got the second win of the season, beating a struggling Theo behind Purdy (31.7) and the Vikings defense (20.0). This team is still last in PF and 2-8,
    but that was a nice week to pad the stats. Unfortunately, even with the big win last week, Purdy Neat is sitting at a 0% chance to make the playoffs.


14. Hayden
    Hayden is officially not going winless, which is huge for team morale. A 113.64 to 84.58 win over Darren, behind Deebo (25.9), Cam Ward (19.1), and Omarion Hampton (17.5), made it 1-9.
    It could have been even better at 78% efficiency, with Mendoza, Perine, McGowan, and Tre' Harris all scoring over 13 on the bench. With Purdy Neat also winning, we'll keep the power rankings
    at the bottom how they are for now. Next up on the revenge tour is Siegel.
