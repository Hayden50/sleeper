POWER RANKINGS

==== Champions ====

1. Brett
    So far there is no reason to move Brett outside of the top spot. Still maintaining an undefeated record, he had a big win over the Cookie Wookies in week 5 and then a little bit of a hangover game in 
    week 6 against Hayden where he put up just 97.5. His team is dealing with some injuries to major players, which will be something to keep an eye on - McLaurin is out and Evans is questionable with 
    Achilles tendinitis. It was interesting that in Brett's first game without Goff and Gibbs he had such a departure from his usual dominance. He'd better hope those guys can stay healthy if he wants
    to keep his winning streak alive.


===== Solid Playoff Teams ======


2. Scott
    Scott has had a good last couple of weeks, getting 2 wins while putting up 137.3 and 136.8. It's nice to see a team scoring so consistently in these midseason weeks where byes and injuries can
    cause some surprising upsets. Part of that consistency comes from the continued success of Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who put up over 20 points in both of those victories. Additionally, seeing DeVonta 
    Smith put up 20.5 in his first game back from injury is a good sign, but it'll be interesting to see what happens now that Hurts will be out for a while.


3. Siegel
    The biggest news of the last couple of weeks is the trade between Goober McGee and Tickle My Kittle, resulting in Siegel getting Derrick Henry, Jaylen Warren, and Juwan Johnson, all while giving up younger talent
    that didn't see the field that often anyway. With these moves, it's clear that Justin is going for the championship this year. The addition of Henry is a strong help, and now with news of Jacobs
    probably getting to see the field again soon, he might have one of the strongest running back rooms in the league. I've moved Justin to the #3 power ranking spot for now, but if he can pull off the win
    against his rival Scott this weekend, he could see himself move into the Champions tier.


4. Jacob
    Jacob took a tough but respectable loss to Brett in week 5, putting up 138.3, and followed that up with a nice win over a somewhat struggling Charles where he put up 140.5. Injuries are still bothering this roster,
    with Rice and Montgomery questionable and, more importantly, Davante Adams landing on IR. With that being said, depth has been the best asset for this team all year, so the hope just has to be that if the Cookie Wookies are dealing
    with injuries, so are all the other teams. The bigger issue might be lineup decisions: Isaiah Likely scored 32.4 on the bench in week 5, and in week 6 Stevenson got the start (3.6) while Loveland put up 18.5 on the bench. With this much depth, picking the right guys matters as much as the injuries.


5. Chandler
    Chandler had a rough week 5 with the lowest score in the league (97.6), but he bounced back nicely in week 6 to start making up for it. In order for him to climb any higher in the rankings, he needs 
    Lamb, Waddle, and Odunze to start playing like the top WR room that they should be. Unfortunately, it seems like so far his WRs are being pulled down by awkward QB situations, and it's unlikely
    that will change much in the coming weeks.


6. Theo
    Theo has had an odd couple of weeks where it seemed that his major players' performances didn't correlate with a win or loss the way you'd expect. In his week 5 loss to Nathan, Puka Nacua put up 37.7, but in his week 6 victory over
    Darren, Bijan Robinson put up just 7.3. Theo's team has continued to be a bit of a disappointment this year - although his team is stacked with talent, the whole squad just doesn't seem to produce the points you'd like to see. Currently
    Theo sits 9th in PF, which is well outside of the playoff tier. Even still, he's held it together with a solid record, and he still has the right guys to turn it around. A win against his rival this weekend would go a long 
    way toward securing him a spot in the playoffs come the end of the regular season.


===== Outside Looking In ======

7. Caleb
    Pitts and Giggles' team is clearly starting to heat up after a rough start to the season. Both of the last 2 weeks have been victories with strong performances - putting up scores of 148.5 and 136.5. Basically all of his 
    major players are producing, with good performances from Pickens, JT, Jeanty, and Lawrence. Ironically, now that Kincaid is in the starting spot, Pitts is finally putting up points and Kincaid is really struggling. This is 
    probably painful, as Kincaid was traded for in a deal that meant Caleb gave up Golden and a first. With Golden killing it right now, it's looking like at least for this season, the trade didn't go Caleb's way.


8. Nathan
    Nathan did have a good win against Theo in week 5, but had a tough loss the week after trading away Warren and Henry. Only putting up 99.6 in a loss to a team that is next to you in the power rankings is not a good sign for the playoffs, but
    trading away Derrick Henry suggests that Nathan may have already given up on that. It's an understandable spot that Nathan is in, and it will be interesting to see if he is able to rebuild effectively or if his time at the top of the 
    league is up for good.



====== Meh =======

    
9. Matt
    Matt posted the highest score over the last 2 weeks with a 174.9-point domination of Darren, but followed it up with a loss to the new and improved Goober McGee lineup in week 6. Unfortunately for Matt, even though he has the 4th-highest PF, he
    is sitting at 2-4 with injuries to McConkey, Breece, Bucky, Warren, McMillan, and Mason. You just can't have consistently good performances when half the team is out, especially during this midseason stretch where other starters are going to be 
    going through bye weeks. It's going to be difficult for Matt to make the playoffs with the current landscape of the league, but it's not impossible with a couple of good wins. He still has Purdy Neat and Saquon My Balls to play this season, which 
    should improve morale a bit.


10. Curran
    Curran has lost both of the last 2 weeks and is now dealing with major injury issues. Achane being out with a torn ACL is a killer for this team, and the bad luck with points against continues to pile on. Along with the injury to Achane,
    Monangai is out and Doubs is on IR. With another match against rival Brett coming up next, I'd expect this team to drop another game and slide to 2-5. The one piece of good news I have is that there is a pretty significant gap between the top
    10 and the bottom 4, so I would be very surprised if things got any worse than this (power-rankings-wise).


===== Future Dynasty / Rebuilders =====


11. Darren
    Even after back-to-back losses, Darren is going to stay as the top member of the "Future Dynasty / Rebuilders" tier (an accolade to be proud of). Yes, the team has real weak spots and is now going to be without QB Jalen Hurts, but the emergence 
    of Shough as a great asset and Nabers as a top WR mean that this team has something to build around, and could maybe get another win or two as the season progresses - especially in this upcoming match against rival Purdy Neat. In all honesty, the
    reason that Darren gets to stay at the 11th power-ranked position has a lot to do with how bleak things are looking for leaguemate Charles.


12. Charles
    There was a glimmer of hope for Charles when he beat Purdy Neat in week 5, but we were brought back to reality in a brutal loss to Jacob where the team put up just 85.8 points. Saquon hasn't played in either game, DJ Moore is now out with a shoulder injury, and Herbert has been benched in favor
    of Brissett and Geno Smith. Charles did make one smart move, sending QB Jalon Daniels to Theo for Theo's 2027 third-round pick. For a roster this old, turning a QB
    he wasn't starting into a future pick is the right direction. Personally, I'd recommend the sell off to continue in order to try to salvage something before some of these old guys retire and lose all value.


13. Jesse
    In an unexpected turn of events, Purdy Neat gets their first bump in the power rankings all season. Realistically, this is more due to external factors than this team's performance, but nonetheless it's exciting to see some more changes at the bottom
    of the rankings. As for Purdy Neat's performance, it has been respectable over weeks 5 and 6, putting up 104.36 and 106.46 respectively. The big players to watch out for on this squad are Tre Tucker and Emeka Egbuka, who are carrying the load while 
    the running back corps deals with injuries. 


14. Hayden
    Things are not looking good for Hayden's team. Coming into the season, there weren't a lot of very startable players on the roster, but since then it's only gotten worse. Most of the players that had high expectations have underperformed - most 
    notably Omarion Hampton - and all of the mid-level guys that are barely starting have gotten hurt. Hayden lost in both week 5 and week 6 while putting up 61.3 in week 6 with 100% starter efficiency - there was literally nothing better on the bench. The big winner from this situation is Purdy 
    Neat, who has Hayden's first-round pick for next year. In a different world, Hayden could be feeling good about the possibility of getting Jeremiah Smith, but it doesn't seem likely in this current situation.
